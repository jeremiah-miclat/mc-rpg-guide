RPG Plugin Encyclopedia

A living reference for every system in the plugin. Gear, Upgrading, World Bosses, and Deviants are fully documented below; more sections land here as they're written up.
Gear section compiled from ItemUtils.java (item & armor definitions), GearSkillListener.java (skill logic, cooldowns, activation triggers), GearInfoGui.java (in‑game /gearinfo lore text) and WorldBossGearDropTable.java (drop pools) — reflects what's implemented, not aspirational text. "RPG" rows are the bonus added on top of the flat vanilla effect, scaled by the stat named (Intelligence, Strength, Vitality share, etc.); "Flat‑only" means the vanilla effect doesn't scale with stats at all.
Upgrading section compiled from RollableStat.java (stat pool & per-level values), GearLevelSystem.java (Level/lore rendering, broken-state lore), GearRegistry.java (Gear Level, socket & broken-flag PDC), AscendingGUI.java/AscendingGUIListener.java (Combine Forge modes, including Repair), GearBreakUtil.java/PlayerDeathListener.java (death-breaks-gear trigger) and GearSkillListener.java's vBase/vSetBase/vAmplifier tiering helpers.
World Bosses section compiled directly from WorldBossSummonListener.java (ritual recipes & summoning), the per-boss skill managers (PiglinBruteSkillManager.java, ZombifiedPiglinSkillManager.java, RavagerSkillManager.java, EvokerSkillManager.java, WardenSkillManager.java/WardenChargeTask.java, WitherFlightController.java), WorldBossTraversalManager.java (shared traversal ability & anti-cheese combat lock), MonsterInitializer.java (level/HP/damage scaling) and DeadMobListener.java (drop chances). The in-game /worldbossguide GUI text was resynced alongside this page — both now cover each boss's full current skill kit, including the four "mass seize" abilities (Molten Manacle, Feeding Frenzy, Resonant Grasp, Tectonic Impale) added since the previous pass.
Deviants section compiled from Deviant.java/DeviantType.java (data model & weapon restrictions), DeviantGenerator.java (stat/skill/element/trait rolls), DeviantStatCalculator.java (every derived-stat formula), DeviantLevelingService.java/AbyssAutoConverter.java (Abyss XP & Level curve), DeviantSkillManager.java (skill effects & cooldowns), DeviantSummonService.java/DeviantSummonCooldown.java (summon limits & cooldowns) and DeviantGuiManager.java (buy/equip GUIs & arena targeting).
